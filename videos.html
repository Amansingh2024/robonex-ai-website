01 / ARDUINO DYNAMIC STABILIZATION ARDUINO ROBOTICS

Arduino Self-Balancing Inverted Pendulum Robot

Watch a dual-wheel rover maintain upright balance automatically using real-time gyroscope and accelerometer readings from an MPU6050 sensor. The Arduino runs high-speed proportional-integral-derivative (PID) algorithms to counter gravity and tilt disturbances.

What RoboNex Students Learn: Students calculate complementary angle filtering, tune PID proportional gains (Kp, Ki, Kd) in real-time serial monitors, and master PWM motor driver responsiveness.

02 / ARDUINO MECHATRONICS & SERVOS PHYSICAL KINEMATICS

Interactive 4-DOF Articulated Robotic Arm

Constructed with high-torque micro-servos and custom mechanical brackets, this 4-degrees-of-freedom robotic arm executes precise pick-and-place trajectories controlled by dual analog potentiometers and joystick modules.

What RoboNex Students Learn: Students map analog input voltages (0–1023) to angular servo degree limits (0–180°), program multi-servo pulse timing without blocking delays, and design balanced mechanical linkages.
▣Arduino Controller 3D4-DOF Acrylic Rig ↻Micro Servos (SG90/MG996R) ⌘Analog Potentiometers

03 / ARDUINO AUTONOMOUS RESCUE EMERGENCY ROBOTICS

Autonomous Fire-Fighting & Extinguisher Rover

An intelligent emergency response rover equipped with a 3-channel infrared flame sensor array. The robot autonomously navigates toward detected heat sources, steers to center the flame, and triggers a 5V submersible water pump to extinguish it.

What RoboNex Students Learn: Students wire optocoupled relays for DC pump switching, calibrate optical wavelength thresholds for fire detection, and implement multi-state emergency avoidance routines.
▣Arduino Uno ◉Triple Flame Array ↔Ultrasonic Obstacle Sonar ⚡5V Submersible Pump

High-Speed Line Tracking & Navigation Rover

Demonstrating precision autonomous ground tracking, this Arduino rover reads high-contrast black lines across reflective surfaces with a multi-channel infrared reflectance array, adjusting wheel PWM speeds instantly for sharp turns.

What RoboNex Students Learn: Students build phototransistor comparator circuits, write proportional line-following logic in C++, and optimize differential motor steering for high-velocity stability.
